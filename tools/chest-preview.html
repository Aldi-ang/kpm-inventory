What is real here, and what is not

  • The motion is copied, the art is not. The lid tilts back and the panel grows out of it in about a quarter second with a small overshoot — the same beat as the video. Every pixel here is drawn by us with plain boxes; no Minecraft texture, sound file or font is used, because those belong to Mojang and cannot ship in a product you sell.
  • The sound is made in the browser, not downloaded. A short wooden creak and a thud, built from a tone and some noise. It can be swapped for a real recording of a crate later — a recording we own.
  • Look 1 keeps the grey stone panel so you can judge it against the video. It breaks your palette rule (grey is not slate, and the frame is not gold). Look 2 is the same panel in KPM colours.
  • The title is the agent's name, as you asked — "Budi Santoso inventory". Switch the name above and the panel follows.
  • Lite Mode and "reduce motion" stop the animation — the panel is simply already open, with no lid tilt. The slots and the numbers still read.

One thing to decide beyond the look: the real agent inventory has more than 27 things in it — products, damaged goods, the pita cukai debt. A chest with 27 slots is a beautiful opening for a van manifest, but it cannot hold a screen. So the chest is the way in: it opens, the panel fills with what the van carries, and the rest of the screen stays below it. That is what this prototype shows.
