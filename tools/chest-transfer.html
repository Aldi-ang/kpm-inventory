    Sound studio

    Every sound in the chests is a real recording from Kenney's RPG Audio pack — free and public domain, so it is safe in a paid app. I picked one for each moment. Change the recording, cut where it starts and stops, set how loud and how high; letting go of a slider plays it. Press Simpan and the chests play your version on every device — and that is the version the app gets.

    Try it

    • Drag a box from the warehouse onto the van — onto any of its squares, or onto the small chest itself. The square it will land in lights up while you carry it. If the van already holds that product, the box always joins its own square: one product is one square and one line.
    • Drag it back. A box in the van goes back to the warehouse the same way: drop it on the warehouse and the panel asks how many come back. Dropped on another square in the van, it only moves there — the arrangement is saved, nothing else changes.
    • The panel starts empty on purpose. You type the number; the line beside it always says what it means in packs (= 600 Bks), worked out with the app's own conversion. More than the warehouse (or the van) holds is refused before you can press anything; Ambil semua / Kembalikan semua fills in everything that can move, and you still confirm it.
    • The goods fly across the middle, from the square they left to the square they land in, one box for each Slop or Bal up to six, with a soft knock as each lands.
    • Nothing is written by a drag. Until MUAT VAN the squares carry +N / −N, and a product that is new to the van has a dashed edge. MUAT VAN writes each product, reports in one line, and prints one surat jalan for what went out and one bukti kembali for what came back.
    • Search finds a product among hundreds. Drop on the damaged row → refused out loud. Lite Mode keeps every box, number and page; only the motion (and the purple specks) stop.
