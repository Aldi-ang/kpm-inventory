    Try it

    • Drag a box from the warehouse onto the van — onto any of its squares, or onto the small chest itself. The square it will land in lights up while you carry it. If the van already holds that product, the box always joins its own square, so one product is one square and one line.
    • The panel starts empty on purpose. You type the number; the line beside it always says what it means in packs (= 600 Bks), worked out with the app's own conversion, so "3" can never be read as 3 packs when you meant 3 Bal. More than the warehouse holds is refused before you can press anything.
    • Nothing is written by a drag. Until MUAT VAN, the warehouse square shows −N and the van square +N with a dashed edge. MUAT VAN writes each product, then reports in one line and makes one surat jalan.
    • Search finds a product among hundreds: the warehouse chest then shows only what matches, and you drag from there.
    • Drop on the damaged row → refused out loud. Drop back on the warehouse → the box goes home and nothing changes. Tap a line in the muatan list to change its amount; × takes it out.
    • Lite Mode keeps every box, number and page; only the motion stops. No keyboard needed either: focus a warehouse box and press Enter.

    About the sound: it is made from the physics of wood — a hinge creak, the lid's knock, a metal latch, a pack landing in a crate. No file from the game is in it. Our notes say you have turned down made sounds three times; if this one is not right either, record a real wooden box lid on your phone and send it — I cut it the way the book sounds were cut.
