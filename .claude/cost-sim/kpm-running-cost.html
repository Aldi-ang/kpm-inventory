What the numbers assume
  • Measured on the emulator on 19 Oct 2026 with the real listener list: one salesman opening the app at 100 people costs 18,986 reads (load-test/hundred-users.mjs).
  • 9 in 10 people are salesmen; each serves about 11 shops. Office users also download the audit log (one line per sale).
  • Half the phones have the app open while sales happen, and every new sale is sent to each of them.
  • Each sale writes 4 records (receipt, van, month tally, audit line); each salesman's day-end adds 3.
  • First opens fall between 06:30 and 08:30; the rest between 09:00 and 19:00; sales between 08:00 and 18:00.
  • The free reads restart at midnight in California, which is 14:00 WIB (15:00 after 2 November).
  • Photo files on Cloud Storage are priced at about US$0.023 per GB a month (estimate, not read from the price page).
