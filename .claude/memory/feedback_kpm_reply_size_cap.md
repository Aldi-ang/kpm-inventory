Aldi, 2026-09-04: *"stop bertele2 just tell me if i need to do something or if there is some
question or input needed by me tell it, i want direct straightforwards information, as always i
want this kind of conversation for every session"*.

("bertele-tele" = rambling, going on and on.)

**A reply contains only two things: what he must DO, and what he must ANSWER.** If there is
neither, the reply is one line saying it's done and nothing is needed.

**And do not stop to report.** Aldi, 2026-09-08: *"keep continue until you need my input, and can u
just give me summary and important points only to tell me so that i dont have to read all of your
description if not needed"*. Finishing a piece of work is not a reason to hand back the turn — keep
going through the queue until something genuinely needs his decision, then ask. When work IS
reported, it is a SUMMARY and the important points, never the account of how it was done.

**Delete before sending — all of it, every time:**
- how the work was done, what was measured, what was traced, what was found
- why a decision was made, what the alternative was, what nearly went wrong
- lists of what shipped, commit hashes, check totals — unless he asked
- "worth knowing", "one thing to note", "for context" — these are all narration
- restating his own request back to him

**Keep:** the ask (🔴 DECIDE / ❓ ANSWER / ✅ TEST), and the one line of context he needs to
answer it. Questions still get plain-English wording and a worked example when the choice is not
obvious — see [[feedback_explain_in_plain_english]]. That carve-out is the ONLY reason to write
more than a few lines.

**Why:** he skims and he decides. Everything else has a home that is not the chat — the commit
message, `PROGRESS.md`, and the A-Brain vault. Measured 12:1 on his own transcripts; see
[[feedback_tell_it_once]]. This supersedes the older "under ~10 lines" phrasing: the limit is not
length, it is CONTENT — a three-line reply that narrates work still breaks it.
