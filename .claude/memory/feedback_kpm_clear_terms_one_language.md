Aldi, 2026-09-01: *"use better language like before also add this learning into your memory, always
use clear terms, not fague one and if the restock vault is in english then this regional warehouse
should be in english as well if indo then indo"*.

Third time on vagueness — *"its so fague"* (2026-08-30, a label that never said less THAN WHAT) and
*"i want u to change tempat into better wording than that, it is so fague"* (2026-08-31, which
became **Data Induk**).

**Why:** a label he cannot read is a screen he cannot use, and he owns the company. And two sibling
screens in two different languages read as two different products — the Restock Vault and the
regional warehouse are the same job at two ends of one shipment.

**How to apply:**

- **Before naming any panel, tab or column, open the SIBLING screen and match it.** Do not decide
  the language from the rule in your head; read what is actually on the other screen.
- **A name must say what it is, in full.** No word that only makes sense if you already know what
  the panel does. Not "Tempat", not "Daftar", not "Minta".
- **Names he has personally chosen never change** — **Data Induk** is his, picked from four options
  on 2026-08-31, and it stays whatever language the rest of the screen is in.
- Offer names, never pick silently — [[project_kpm_ui_rework_phases]] and his own locked law that
  only he names the categories in his trade.

⚠️ **The Restock Vault is NOT consistently either language** (measured 2026-09-01): English for
Batch No, Date, Extra Costs, Qty Received, Select Product, Metadata, Shipping/Labor/Tax (Rp),
Target Month; Indonesian for Barang, Jenis, Jumlah, Nama, Nota, Kirim, Hapus, Proses, Tanggal,
Ongkos kirim, Pita cukai; and three fields carry both — "Nama Pengirim (Sender Name)", "Nomor Resi
/ Tracking No", "Surat Jalan / Delivery No". So "match the sibling" had no clean answer and the
mixture had to be put to him rather than guessed at.
