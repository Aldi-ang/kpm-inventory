Every question for Aldi, and every decision only he can make, must carry a visible emoji
marker so it survives skimming. Asked for directly, 2026-08-05:

> "can u do highlight for important information or question that i need to fullfill, just
> put some emoji or something if its something i must decide or questions for me, i dont
> think i will read them all, and i might miss some of your request"

**Why:** this is the second time the same problem has been raised — see
[[feedback-lead-with-the-ask]], where he said *"always highlight your question or job that i
need to do for u because i wont read your whole thinking message."* The bold opening line
was not enough on its own, because a long reply can contain several asks and only the first
one was getting marked.

**How to apply:**
- `🔴 DECIDE:` — a blocking choice only he can make
- `❓ ANSWER:` — a question that changes what gets built
- `✅ TEST:` — something he has to check in the running app
- Keep the single bold opening line as well; the markers are in addition, not instead.
- If a reply contains no marker, say explicitly that nothing is needed from him.
- Group them at the top or the bottom when there is more than one, so they are never
  stranded in the middle of an explanation.
