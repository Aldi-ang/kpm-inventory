When I need Aldi to decide between design options, I show him rendered frames or a mockup of each option with a one-line plain caption — never a prose description of what the options are.

**Why:** his words, 2026-09-14, after a phone-layout options list written as a table of class names: *"if u want me to decide then give me ilustration or image or anything to show me what is the recommended changes instead of telling me with your technical AI sentence that i wont understand keep this work method in mind when working with me"*. He also said what the goal is, so no option list is needed for it: *"we need all the panel whatever the method is, i just want to make sure that all the features is available on the phone as good as pc uses"*.

**How to apply:** for any 🔴 DECIDE on a screen, render the current state and each candidate at the real width (lab + `?css=` preview override, or his Chrome device-toolbar frames), compose them side by side with the option name and one plain sentence each, and send the image (SendUserFile) or publish an artifact. The recommendation is marked ON the image. Text in the reply is only the question and the numbers. Same rule as [[feedback-explain-in-plain-english]] and [[feedback-diagnose-before-changing]] — this is the visual half of both.
