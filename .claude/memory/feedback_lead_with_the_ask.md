Aldi told me twice (2026-08-02, second time after it was missed) to **put the question or the
job he has to do at the very top of the reply**, highlighted. His words: *"always highlight
your question or job that i need to do for u because i wont read your whole thinking message."*

**Why:** he skims. He is 14, running a production app, and often reading on a phone between
other work. A question placed after the reasoning is a question he never sees — which stalls
the whole task while I sit waiting for an answer he does not know I asked for. This already
cost a turn where the Lite-Mode-vs-light-theme question went unanswered because it sat at the
bottom of a long reply.

**How to apply:** open every reply with ONE bold line stating what he must do — answer a
question, run a command, test something, pick between options. If nothing is needed from him,
say "nothing for you to do" in one line. Everything else — reasoning, verification, evidence,
confidence tags — goes below it. Never bury an ask.

since a memory only helps if it is recalled and that file always is.
