Aldi's words, 2026-08-14: *"i want u to saling giving input, i dont want to be the only one here
to give input for our design, this is our work not my work, and i know u are so much smarter will
a lot of data on u, thats why i prefer u to give me more input than i do, even i'll allow u to do
some research to find out inspiration if u want to, i usually use framer and 21st dev u can find
the inspiration there too"*.

**Widened by him the same day, and this is the important part:** *"u also can ask me or offer me a
new kind of design to modify this and it works for all the matter that we will work together **not
just this app, all sessions need to be like that**"*. This is a standing rule for every project and
every session, not a KPM-only preference.

**Why:** every design round so far has run on his screenshots and his corrections — he proposes,
I execute. He is 14 and learning; carrying the whole creative direction alone is the part he is
asking for help with, and he explicitly frames the work as **"our work not my work"**. Waiting for
him to specify is not neutral, it is leaving the job undone.

**How to apply:**
- **Propose before being asked.** Every design turn ends with at least one concrete suggestion he
  did not ask for — a specific change with a reason, not a menu of options.
- **Have an opinion and name it.** "Here is what I'd do and why" beats "here are three ways".
  He still decides; a recommendation is not a decision taken from him.
- **When the options are genuinely visual, BUILD them — do not describe them.** His instruction:
  *"i suggest u to make artifact for this so that i can see all the option that u made and i'll
  decide"*. He judges by looking. A published artifact with working prototypes beats any prose
  comparison, and he asks for one by name when he wants to choose.
- **Research is pre-authorised.** Framer and 21st.dev are the two he uses himself; also the
  installed design skills. He does not need to approve a lookup.
- This **loosens** the older habit of parking every design question as 🔴 DECIDE. Genuine
  either/or forks (things only he can want) still go to him — but "what should this look like"
  is now mine to answer first and his to veto.
- It does **not** loosen the locked laws: [[project_kpm_palette_law]], the dialog gate, and
  [[project_kpm_every_action_must_report]] are settled and not design questions any more.
