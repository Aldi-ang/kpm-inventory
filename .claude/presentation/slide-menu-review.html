Slide Menu Review

Four reviewers tried to break the new slide menu (the round button at the bottom of each slide). Four checkers then re-tested every report from scratch and tried to prove it wrong. None were thrown out. Several were the same problem seen from two or three sides, so it comes to 11 problems. All 11 are fixed and live in the presentation.

On a phone what you or a customer would actually hit

  1. Swiping up on the open menu didn't scroll the slide

    medium
    What
    With the menu open, a thumb on it swiping up to read further did nothing. The bottom of the screen was a dead zone until the menu folded.
    Now
    The menu takes only sideways swipes. Up and down scroll the slide as normal.

  2. Tapping the dot just as the menu folds jumped to another slide

    lowseen 2×
    What
    For about a third of a second while folding, the fading slide buttons still caught taps. A quick tap on the dot could open a different slide, or do nothing. It's hard to hit, but it happens on PC too.
    Now
    The folding part ignores taps the moment it starts folding, so the tap always reaches the dot.

  3. The menu folded while a finger was still on it

    low
    What
    A finger resting on the strip didn't count as touching, so it folded away under the finger.
    Now
    It stays open while a finger is on it, then folds 2.5 seconds after the finger lifts.

  4. The opening bounce went past the screen edges on landscape phones

    lowseen 2×
    What
    The spring overshoots by about 10%. Turned sideways, the menu poked 12 to 18 pixels off both edges for a moment.
    Now
    The bounce keeps its feel but stops 4 pixels inside each edge.

  5. The menu opened by itself again after it was closed

    low
    What
    On the very first slide, the one-time "peek" could reopen a menu that had just been closed.
    Now
    Once the dot has been used, the peek is cancelled.

With a mouse presenting from the PC

  1. After clicking the menu, any key press kept it open forever

    low
    What
    A click left focus in the menu. Pressing an arrow key or Page Down afterwards told the menu "someone is using the keyboard here", so it never folded.
    Now
    Mouse clicks no longer leave focus in the menu. It folds as normal.

With a keyboard Tab, Enter and Esc users

  1. You couldn't see when the dot was selected

    mediumseen 3×
    What
    The gold selection ring was drawn outside the dot, where the menu cuts it off, so the ring was completely hidden.
    Now
    The ring is drawn just inside the dot.

  2. Tab could land on the invisible dot while the menu was open

    lowseen 2×
    What
    The dot fades out when the menu opens, but Tab could still reach it. Focus then vanished and the menu stayed open.
    Now
    While the menu is open, Tab skips the dot.

  3. Leaving a slide with the keyboard lost your place

    lowseen 2×
    What
    Going back to the wheel with Esc or the gold button dropped focus to the top of the page, so the arrow keys and Enter stopped working.
    Now
    Focus lands on "Buka", so Enter opens the slide again.

  4. Tabbing through the slides left the selected one half-hidden

    low
    What
    Every second or third slide button sat half under the faded edge, with its ring cut off.
    Now
    The strip slides along so the selected button is always fully in view.

  5. Esc then a quick Tab landed on the vanishing home button

    low
    What
    For a moment after folding, Tab could reach the gold button as it faded away, and then focus was lost.
    Now
    The folded part can't be reached at all, even mid-fold.

How it was proven

20 new automatic checks test exactly these 11 problems on a landscape phone, a normal phone and a PC, with real finger touches, mouse clicks and key presses. They pass twice in a row. All the older checks still pass too: the slide menu (55), the wheel (33), the slides (36), the opening (32), earlier fixes (15) and the terminal (7), plus the headline, button and overlap layout checks.
